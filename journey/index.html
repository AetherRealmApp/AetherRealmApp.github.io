Core Journey

The path is short. The history is not.

REALM 1.2.3 is built around one daily cycle. Features that do not exist in the app are not described here.

AWAKEN

Wake the Core

Once the cycle is ready, AWAKEN starts a new 24-hour Core. It is the daily Core awakening ritual.

DAY

Your day on the path

DAY is your day on the REALM journey. The Grove keeps that history as you return.

ESSENCE

Progression

Essence is earned through AWAKEN and recorded in your history — the measure of how far your Realm has come.

STREAK

Return tomorrow

Streak measures consecutive return. Missing a cycle breaks the streak. REALM does not invent extra streak games on this page.

AETHER

A presence that changes

Aether is your evolving presence in Living Grove. It changes as days, Essence and milestones accumulate. The original Aether look remains free.

GROVE

Living Grove

The free starter Realm. Bundled with the app, and the world every REALM account begins in.

EVOLUTION
