Account & Data Deletion

REALM · app.aetherrealm.realm · 1.2.2 (5)

Use this page to request deletion of a REALM account even if the Android app is no longer on your phone.

In-app deletion

REALM 1.2.2 does not provide an in-app account deletion action. Profile includes sign-out only. Sign-out ends the session on that device; it does not delete the cloud account. That missing in-app control is a Google Play Data safety / account deletion gap until an app update adds it. This website does not change the Android app.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the deletion request form (GitHub Issues on this public website repository). A GitHub account is required because REALM does not publish a support email.
  2. Include the Google account email you used to sign in to REALM, your REALM username if you have one, and whether you used Google Play REALM (app.aetherrealm.realm) or REALM EA (app.aetherrealm.realm.web).
  3. We verify that the request matches an account we can identify, then delete the account data listed below. There is no automated self-serve API for this in 1.2.2.

Open deletion request

The form is public by default. Do not put passwords or backup files in the issue. Email is used only to find the account.

What is deleted

  • REALM cloud account linked to that Google identity (user id, username, referral code).
  • Authentication session for REALM at the backend.
  • Recovery snapshot, server Essence / referral Essence, AWAKEN records tied to the account.
  • Public ranking rows that display your username.
  • Premium entitlement rows for that account, if any exist.

What may be retained

  • Anonymous operational logs needed for security, for a short period, without your username.
  • Referral edges on other accounts (that someone was referred) may be kept as an anonymous count so other users’ history does not rewrite. Your username is not kept on those rows after deletion.
  • Records we must keep if the law requires it.
  • Files you exported yourself, and copies on your devices, are outside REALM cloud. Uninstall or clear app data to remove local REALM storage on a phone you still have.

How the process works

Requests are handled manually. After we can match the Google identity to a REALM account, we delete the cloud data above. We aim to complete this within 30 days of a verifiable request. You will receive a reply on the GitHub issue when it is done, unless you asked us not to comment publicly — in that case close the issue after filing and we still process the request.

Google may retain sign-in records under Google’s own policy. Uninstalling REALM from Android does not tell Google to delete your Google account.
