Account & Data Deletion

REALM · app.aetherrealm.realm · 1.2.3 (6)

Delete a REALM account from the Android app, or use this page if the app is no longer on your phone. Signing out is not account deletion.

In-app deletion

REALM 1.2.3 includes Delete Account / Hesabımı Sil on the Profile screen, next to Sign out. The action requires two confirmations. It cannot be done with a single tap. The warning states that the action cannot be undone, that your REALM account and cloud progress are deleted, and that signing out does not delete the account.

  1. Open REALM 1.2.3 (app.aetherrealm.realm) and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account / Hesabımı Sil. Read the first warning, then Continue.
  3. Confirm a second time. The app sends a signed request for your own account only.
  4. If the server succeeds, local REALM progress is cleared, the session ends, and you return to sign-in. If it fails, your session and local data stay so you can try again.

Request deletion from this page

Use this private form if you no longer have the app. Your Google account email is sent only to REALM’s backend. It is not posted as a public GitHub issue.

Do not put passwords, backup files, or authentication tokens in this form. We match the request to an account we can identify, then delete the data listed below. We aim to complete web requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • REALM cloud account linked to that Google identity (user id, username, referral code).
  • Authentication user and REALM session at the backend.
  • Recovery snapshot, server Essence / referral Essence belonging to that user, AWAKEN records tied to the account.
  • Public ranking identity that displayed your username.
  • Premium entitlement and purchase rows for that account, if any exist.
  • In the app path: local REALM progress and cached account state on that device after the server succeeds.

What may be retained

  • Anonymous operational logs needed for security, for a short period, without your username.
  • Referral edges on other accounts (that someone was referred) may be kept so other users’ earned history is not rewritten. Your username is not kept on those rows after deletion.
  • Records we must keep if the law requires it.
  • Files you exported yourself, and copies on your devices, are outside REALM cloud. Uninstall or clear app data to remove local REALM storage on a phone you still have.

How the process works

In-app deletion is processed immediately for the signed-in user. The server uses the authenticated session; another person’s user id cannot be supplied by the client. Web requests from this page are stored privately and handled after we can match the Google identity to a REALM account. Signing in again with the same Google account after a successful deletion creates a new empty REALM account; previous cloud progress does not return.

Google may retain sign-in records under Google’s own policy. Uninstalling REALM from Android does not tell Google to delete your Google account.
